About Me

I'm a full stack developer at Polywood, where I build and maintain the company's internal software, from MySQL databases and PHP and Java back ends to React front ends. I also manage development projects from the first requirements conversation through release.

I earned a B.S. in Robotics Engineering Technology from Purdue University in 2023, and I've been building robots and writing software for more than ten years. In Purdue's Introduction to Robotics (MFET 24800) and Advanced Industrial Robotics (MFET 34800) courses, I worked hands-on with industrial 6-axis and SCARA robots, and through Purdue SIGbots I built and programmed autonomous mobile robots. In 2023 I started Loosey Goosey Robotics, a casual robotics competition, and I wrote the tournament software it runs on.
